Teacher’s quick guide

1. Model one answer with a reason (2 minutes). 2. Play the five stops, saying each answer aloud (8–10 minutes). 3. Interview a partner using this sheet (5 minutes). 4. Share a suitable suggestion (2 minutes).

Check: a complete answer; correct by/on/in/at/with; a reason using because; should/could followed by a base verb. Accept different reasonable preferences and reasons. Game models are examples, not the only correct answers.

Practice has no time limit. Challenge has a 30-second target per stop and continues into overtime. All correct labels fall once per round (4, 5, 7, 4, and 3); caught and missed labels both count toward ending the round. Catching words checks categories, not pronunciation or speaking quality. No microphone or automatic speaking assessment is used.
